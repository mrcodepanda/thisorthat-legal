Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Trendi Studios handles information in ThisOrThat - Photo Cleaner.

Photo Library Access

ThisOrThat asks for access to your photo library so it can find and compare duplicate, blurry, and similar photos. Photo analysis happens on your device. We do not upload, collect, sell, or share your photos.

Data Collection

ThisOrThat does not require an account and does not collect personal information from you. The app stores basic cleanup usage information on your device to support the free daily cleanup limit.

Purchases

ThisOrThat offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple's in-app purchase system. Purchases, billing, refunds, and subscription management are handled by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

Analytics and Advertising

ThisOrThat does not currently use third-party analytics SDKs or advertising SDKs.

Children's Privacy

ThisOrThat is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Trendi Studios at trendistudiosofficial@gmail.com. You can also visit the ThisOrThat Support page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy if the app's features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page.
